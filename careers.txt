Our Employee Promise: Create Your Own Sunshine
We are Sun, a community that outshines! In our journey, everyone is enabled to take charge in an environment that offers limitless growth opportunities. We are with you every step of the way, so you can shine for years to come.
Sun was founded on the premise that everyone has potential. We believe this potential can be channelled if we recognise people’s passion for growth, give them a voice and empower them. Our growth and success over the last four decades has been driven by a diverse & multicultural workforce of over 43,000. We have created an ecosystem where you can “Create your own sunshine.”
At the foundation of our employee promise to “Create your own sunshine” lie three pillars – Better Everyday, Take Charge and Thrive Together, so that people can achieve what they would have thought impossible.
At Sun, no role is beyond reach. Our scale and global footprint are a springboard for a long and fulfilling career, powered by continuous learning, meritocratic culture and a variety of experiences to make you a future-ready leader.
We are lifelong learners – like sponges, always eager to absorb knowledge from anyone, at any time, driven by the humility to acknowledge that we don't have to know it all. We take a deep dive into every challenge, approaching problems hands-on to uncover their root causes. Our culture fosters a spirit of experimentation, encouraging us to explore new paths and solve problems creatively. We are given the space to make mistakes, learn from them, and come back stronger. This mindset enables us to quickly learn, adapt, and evolve every day.
At Sun, discover your space to shine. Experience possibilities and never be constrained by the boundaries of your role. Make bold yet thoughtful decisions, knowing that your new ideas are what drive us forward.
How you choose to grow at Sun as you begin your journey is something that you decide. The organisation empowers you. If you're eager to dive into areas you know little about, we'll give you the platform and support you need. Many who joined Sun at entry-level positions are now leading key portfolios—driven by their passion and empowered to learn and grow. We've always believed in showing our people the bigger picture and making them a part of it. Take on big responsibilities, make decisions, and take ownership in your sphere of work. Help is always close at hand, should you need it.
At Sun, we enable you to perform your best. Belong to a team that backs you in every situation. Thrive in a work environment that fosters support, professionalism, and transparency.
Our philosophy is centred around inspiring and encouraging each other. By reaching out to others and offering them help, irrespective of 'what's in it for me'. By striving to create a meritocratic environment where it's your work that speaks for you. At Sun, 'Performance Speaks.' The best part? You'll rarely hear anyone say, "I did this". More often than not, it is always, 'We did this". We've built a community where collaboration and authenticity matter and emotional connections thrive at every level of the organisation, from top to bottom.
At Sun Pharma, we have found strength in our diversity, imbued by our culture of mutual respect, empathy, and adhering to the highest ethical standards. Our approach and culture is represented through Sunology: the Sun Pharma way of life.
Humility, Integrity, Passion & Innovation together form Sunology – a combination of two words, Sun & Ideology. We encourage our employees to personify these ideologies, as they form the cornerstone of everything we create and value as an organisation. We are proud that our global workforce is bound together by our common values of quality, reliability, consistency, trust, and innovation. These values form the core of our culture and steer us towards our vision.
